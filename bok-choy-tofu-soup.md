# \centering\huge\sf Bok Choy Tofu Soup\rm\Huge（白菜豆腐汤）

Credit: Omnivore's Cookbook

Time to prepare: 15 minutes. 

---

\begin{multicols}{2}

\section{\sf Ingredients}

\begin{itemize}
    \item 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
    \item 4 slices ginger
    \item 2 green onions, sliced
    \item 2 cloves garlic, smashed
    \item 2 teaspoons light soy sauce
    \item 8 cups of bok choy (Napa cabbage), sliced to bite-size piecesa (separate the white parts from the green)
    \item 5 ounces mushrooms, sliced to bite-size pieces
    \item \liningnums{\sfrac{1}{2}} to 1 block soft tofu, sliced
    \item Salt to Taste 
    \item \liningnums{\sfrac{1}{4}} teaspoon white pepper 
    \item 1 teaspoon sesame oil
\end{itemize}


\vfill\null

\columnbreak

\section{\sf Preparation}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Heat broth over medium high heat. Add ginger, green onions, garlic, and soy sauce.
    \item When broth comes to boil, add the white parts of the cabbage and the mushrooms. Cook for 4 minutes, until the whites of the cabbages start to soften. 
    \item Add the green parts of the cabbage and the tofu. Continue cooking for 2-3 minutes. 
    \item Add white pepper and drizzle sesame oil. Salt to taste. 
    \item Serve with white rice.
\end{enumerate}
