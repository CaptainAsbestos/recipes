# \centering\huge\sf Korean Drug Eggs\Huge（마약계란）

Credit: Rice Sprinkles on Instragram

Time to prepare: 8 hours

---

\begin{multicols}{2}

\section{\sf Ingredients}

\begin{itemize}
    \item 12 eggs
    \item 3 green onions, chopped
    \item 3 cloves garlic, sliced 
    \item 2-3 thai red chilis, sliced
    \item 2 tablespoons sesame seed
    \item \liningnums{\sfrac{1}{2}} cup soy sauce
    \item 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce 
    \item \liningnums{\sfrac{3}{4}} cup water 
    \item 1 tablespoon sesame oil
\end{itemize}


\vfill\null

\columnbreak

\section{\sf Preparation}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Prepare an ice bath for the eggs.
    \item Boil eggs to desired doneness -- 6-7 minutes for soft boiled, 8 for medium. 
    \item Peel eggs once cooked and cooled in ice bath. 
    \item  In a tupperware container, mix green onions, garlic, chilis, sesame seeds, soy sauces, water, and sesame oil to create marinade.
    \item Add whole peeled eggs to marinade. 
    \item Place paper towerl over eggs to keep them submerged in marinade.
    \item Let eggs marinate for at least 8 hours overnight in fridge.
    \item Enjoy as a snack with rice for a light meal.
\end{enumerate}
